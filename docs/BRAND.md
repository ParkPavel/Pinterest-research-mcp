## Direction and provenance

Two offset editorial frames, warm ivory on graphite: selection, collections and source inspection. No text in the icon, no Pinterest P, red badge, pushpin, platform mark, model-generated Pin imagery or claim of partnership. The icon was generated with the built-in image generator; raster exports were resized/compressed and the banner uses deterministic typography. The raster master is not represented as a hand-drawn vector or guaranteed unique trademark.

Generation brief: standalone square independent visual-research icon; two bold interlocking open rectangular frames; graphite #202522 and ivory #F6F5F1; generous safe margins; legible at small sizes; no letters, Pinterest symbols, red, gradients, mockups or watermark. Image generation can introduce minor texture; inspect actual exports rather than assuming exact pixel colors from the prompt.
